Cancellation and refunds

Effective October 19, 2026

Price and renewal

CallRecTz Premium costs ₪32 per year (final price), paid once a year. The license does not renew automatically: before it ends we email you a link to renew.

How to cancel

Cancel at any time, in any of these ways:

  • Simply do not renew: nothing is charged without your action.
  • By email, for a refund within the period below.
  • By email to atzedek@gmail.com.

Premium keeps working until the end date of the license you paid for.

Refunds

Within 14 days of a purchase or a renewal, you can ask for a full refund for any reason by email. The license key is then revoked. After that no partial refund is given for the current year, except where the law requires otherwise. Refunds go back to the original payment method.

Customers in Israel also have the rights set out in the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, including cancellation of a distance transaction under section 14C and cancellation of an ongoing transaction under section 13D. This policy does not limit those rights.

Your recordings

Cancelling or refunding never deletes or locks recordings you already made.
