Cancellation and refunds

Effective October 19, 2026

Price and renewal

CallRecTz Premium costs $10 per year, plus any sales tax or VAT that applies in your country. The subscription renews automatically every year at the same price until you cancel. We will tell you in advance of any price change.

How to cancel

Cancel at any time, in any of these ways:

  • In the app: Settings, Premium, Manage or cancel subscription.
  • Through the "Manage subscription" link in your Lemon Squeezy receipt email.
  • By email to atzedek@gmail.com.

Cancellation stops the next renewal. Premium keeps working until the end of the year you already paid for.

Refunds

Within 14 days of your first purchase or of a yearly renewal, you can ask for a full refund for any reason by email or through Lemon Squeezy. After that, cancellation stops future renewals and no partial refund is given for the current year, except where the law requires otherwise. Refunds are issued by Lemon Squeezy to the original payment method.

Customers in Israel also have the rights set out in the Consumer Protection Law, 5741-1981, including cancellation of a distance transaction under section 14C and cancellation of an ongoing transaction under section 13D. This policy does not limit those rights.

Your recordings

Cancelling or refunding never deletes or locks recordings you already made.
