Your calls. With you.

CallRecTz is an Android app that records your phone calls automatically, both sides, and keeps the recordings on your phone. No cloud, no ads, no root.

  • Playback, search, sharing and deletion

Premium

  • Everything in Free

Yearly subscription, renews automatically, cancel anytime. One license works on up to 2 phones.

How buying works

Payment is handled by Lemon Squeezy, our reseller and merchant of record, which also issues your receipt and charges any applicable sales tax or VAT. After payment you receive a license key by email. Paste it in the app under Settings, Premium. Recordings you already made never lock, even if your subscription ends.

Requirements

Android 12 or newer. A one time setup activates the built-in recording engine through Android's wireless debugging. Recording quality and availability depend on the phone model and Android version.
