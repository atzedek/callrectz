How buying works

Payment is handled by Grow, an Israeli payment provider, and you receive a receipt by email. After payment we send you a license key by email, usually within a few hours. Paste it in the app under Settings, Premium. Recordings you already made never lock, even after your license ends.

Requirements

Android 12 or newer. A one time setup activates the built-in recording engine through Android's wireless debugging. Recording quality and availability depend on the phone model and Android version.
